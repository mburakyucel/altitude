What every card obeys

Context — the situation, ≤ 2 plain sentences, ≤ 320 chars.
Question — one sentence, ≤ 280 chars, saying what each answer does.
Options — short verbs, ≤ 80 chars; the recommended one first, drawn primary.
In front of the question — no ids, file names or code; those live behind Why, where ids open the ledger.
Revise waits for feedback; a note with Approve travels with the answer, binding. Blocked cards are on the Inbox board.

Build the reader and probe now, so the queue can pause itself in time?

Why ▾
Feedback — required for Revise, optional otherwise
Approve Revise Reject
Reader: harvest the rate-limit event from every headless Claude stream (altitude/quota.py); a reading older than 600 s is stale and does not count. Probe: one Haiku turn, about 0.1% of the window, skipped while a reading is under 10 minutes old. Critic's conditions: (a) stale never counts as fresh; (b) a probe failure is an incident, never a silent fallback (decision 36); (c) the reader writes monitor/quota-codex.json and nothing else. Refs: I-007 · decision 31 · decision 45 · R-003 — spend: M envelope, ≤ 8 launches, about 1% of today's window.
Why open — reasoning, the critic's conditions, file names and ids; ids are links to the ledger. Nothing here is needed to answer.
