What stays visible and what opens
  • The project/task name, current activity, messages, microphone and send arrow stay visible. Text stays 16px; controls are at least 44px.
  • Tap ⋯ for last-answer time, engine choice, task counts, and existing project/task actions. A non-Auto engine pin stays named in the header. The task title opens its complete details.
  • The current task’s question, choices, receipts, Latest messages and View question stay in their existing conversation positions. Needs you counts remain available from the header when navigation is hidden.
  • Update details expand on request. Restart remains visible when available; actionable failures and Retry remain visible. No permanent keyboard-shortcut or delivery-hint row.
  • Try ⋯ and Dismiss keyboard in the proposal. These boards do not send messages, record audio, or run operational actions.
