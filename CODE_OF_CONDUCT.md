# Code of conduct

Altitude is developed in the open, and everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and
other project spaces is welcome regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Disagree about ideas, not people; give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

To report conduct to the maintainer, open an issue saying only that you have a conduct report and
how the maintainer can reach you privately; put no details in the issue. To report a specific
comment, issue or account to GitHub as well, use **Report content** in its menu. Reports are
handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break
this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. This code applies in all project spaces and
when someone represents the project elsewhere.
